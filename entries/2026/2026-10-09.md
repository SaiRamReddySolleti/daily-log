# `gs.eventQueue` payload limits and how people work around them badly

*2026-10-09 00:02 UTC*

`gs.eventQueue(name, record, parm1, parm2)` only gives you two string slots, and both `parm1` and `parm2` on `sys_event` cap out at 4000 characters. Nothing enforces that at the API level — the field just truncates silently on insert. No error, no warning in the logs. You find out when a script action downstream calls `JSON.parse` on a payload that got cut mid-key.
