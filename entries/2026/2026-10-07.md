# Event queue vs Flow Designer triggers

*2026-10-07 23:55 UTC*

Flow Designer's record trigger is not the same mechanism as `gs.eventQueue()`, even though both end up asynchronous. An event queued with `gs.eventQueue()` fires once, with the condition checked at the moment you call it, and whatever payload you passed stays frozen. A Flow Designer trigger re-evaluates its condition twice: once synchronously when the triggering business rule runs (order ~1000, after the record commits), and again when the flow engine actually dequeues and executes the flow. If another transaction changes the record between those two points, the second check can fail silently. No error, no log entry in the flow's execution history — you just get a row in `sys_flow_context` with state `cancelled`, and most people never look there because they're watching `sys_flow` or `ECC Queue` for problems.
